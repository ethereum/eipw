preamble-title-case

The title preamble header is in title case.

Examples

error[preamble-title-case]: preamble header `title` should be in title case
 --> eip-1.md:3:12
  |
3 | title: SSZ transaction signature scheme
  |            ^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^^^
  |
  = help: capitalize every word, except these when not first or last: a, across, after, against, an, and, are, as, at, before, between, but, by, during, for, from, in, into, is, like, nor, not, of, on, only, onto, or, over, per, than, the, through, to, under, upon, via, vs, with, within, without

Explanation

preamble-title-case ensures that every word in the title front matter (or preamble) field starts with an uppercase letter. For a hyphenated word, only the first part has to be capitalized.

The following are exempt, and are never reported:

  • Small words (like of, for, or the), unless they are the first or last word of the title. The list of small words is configurable.
  • Identifiers, which are words that contain a digit, an underscore, a slash, or an uppercase letter after their first character (like secp256k1, eth_call, eth/70, or ecRecover.)
  • Anything inside a code span delimited by backticks.
  • Words that are explicitly allowed in the lint's configuration.

EIP-1 requires titles to be in title case. Capitalizing titles consistently makes lists of proposals easier to scan.
